FEATURES

Built Different

⌨️

Phonetic Transliteration

Type Roman letters, get Devanāgarī. The trie-backed engine handles consonant clusters, virama placement, and vowel matras automatically.

ā

Full IAST Support

All 54 IAST diacritics — ā, ī, ū, ṛ, ṝ, ḷ, ḹ, ś, ṣ, ḥ, ṃ, ṁ, ṭ, ḍ, ṇ, ñ, ṅ — available via flick or dedicated IAST mode.

🧠

Adaptive Learning

The keyboard tracks your vocabulary. Frequently used Sanskrit words surface first in suggestions, personalised per-user via on-device storage.

🔒

Fully On-Device

Zero network requests. All transliteration, suggestion ranking, and vocabulary learning runs in-process. No text ever leaves your device.

🪶

Flick Input

Hold any key and release upward to type its alternate character — IAST diacritics, numeric shortcuts, or rare Vedic phonemes.

🎤

Voice Dictation

Speak Hindi or Sanskrit and watch Devanāgarī appear. Powered by SFSpeechRecognizer with hi-IN locale, fully offline-capable on modern devices.
